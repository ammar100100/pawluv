Frequently Asked Questions

Can I include more than one pet?

Yes. Pawluv products can be configured for one to four pets where the product supports it.

Can I upload my own product photos?

Yes. Use the Admin Dashboard to add product images, descriptions and prices. Product images are then used throughout the shop.

Will I see my design before production?

Yes. Our custom flow is designed around a preview-and-approval step before manufacturing.

How long does an order take?

Production and shipping timing depends on the selected product and current schedule. The expected timing can be confirmed with the order.
